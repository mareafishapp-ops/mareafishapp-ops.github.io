MAREA — Read the Ocean

MAREA reads the ocean for anglers.

It brings together satellite sea temperature, chlorophyll, seabed depth, charted structure and marine forecasts on one map. It then points out water that matches what your target species is known to prefer, and it explains why. MAREA is not a weather app with a fishing skin. It is built around one question: is this water worth checking?

FIND FISH: HABITAT, EXPLAINED

Choose a species, or All species, and a fishing mode (Shore, Inshore or Offshore). MAREA checks the area on your screen against published temperature and depth preferences, temperature fronts, chlorophyll and seabed structure. It marks the cells that fit best. Tap a marker and "Why here" shows each ingredient, where it came from and how old it is. Each result has a 0–100 habitat index. The index compares conditions with a species' known preferences. It is not a probability that fish are present, and MAREA never promises fish. Treat the markers as places worth a closer look.

SEA TEMPERATURE THAT TELLS YOU ITS AGE

  • NOAA GOES-19 satellite sea surface temperature, updated through the day across the Americas, plus global NASA MUR and NOAA Geo-polar analyses
  • The observation time and age are always on screen
  • Cloud gaps stay visible as gaps. They are never filled with made-up values
  • Temperature-front and isotherm lines, auto or fixed colour scale, °C or °F

WATER COLOUR AND CHLOROPHYLL

Satellite chlorophyll from NOAA VIIRS and Copernicus Sentinel-3, with chlorophyll-break lines to help find colour edges and blue water.

STRUCTURE AND DEPTH

  • Seabed depth shading, depth contours and slope (NOAA ETOPO 2022)
  • Charted wrecks and obstructions, and charted restricted areas (NOAA charts, U.S. waters)
  • Named undersea features worldwide (GEBCO) and Florida artificial reefs (FWC)
  • A depth-profile tool to see the drop-off between two points

CONDITIONS

  • Wind, waves, swell and modelled currents (Open-Meteo, Copernicus Marine), with forecast times
  • Measured surface currents from HF radar and NOAA buoy observations where available
  • Tide predictions (NOAA, and SEMAR in parts of Mexico), sun and moon times

ON THE WATER

  • Your GPS position on the map while the app is open, with distances to structures and spots
  • Save spots and waypoints on your phone
  • Tap anywhere for the cell's temperature, depth and nearest buoy, each with its source

HONEST BY DESIGN

Every value is labelled MEASURED, MODELLED or DERIVED, with its source and time. Missing data stays missing, and old data is shown as old.

PRIVACY

No account, no ads, no analytics. Location is used only while MAREA is open. MAREA does fetch data for the area you view, or your position, from the scientific data providers. See the privacy policy.

GOOD TO KNOW

  • MAREA needs an internet connection. Satellite files can be large, so Wi-Fi is recommended for long sessions.
  • Coverage varies. Some layers exist only in certain regions, and species are limited to their known ranges.
  • Satellites cannot see through cloud, and forecasts can be wrong.
  • MAREA is not a navigation chart or a safety tool. Always check official charts, marine forecasts and local fishing regulations, including closed and protected areas.

Data: NOAA, NASA, E.U. Copernicus Marine Service, Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0), OpenStreetMap contributors and others. MAREA is independent and not endorsed by these organizations.
