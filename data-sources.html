Data sources and attribution

MAREA uses public scientific data. Values may be delayed, incomplete or inaccurate. Not for navigation.

  • Sea temperature: NOAA NESDIS GOES-19 ABI SST (NOAA Open Data Dissemination); NASA JPL MUR SST v4.1 (doi:10.5067/GHGMR-4FJ04); NOAA Geo-polar Blended SST — via NOAA CoastWatch ERDDAP.
  • Chlorophyll: NOAA NESDIS STAR / CoastWatch VIIRS; Generated using E.U. Copernicus Marine Service Information (doi:10.48670/moi-00278).
  • Currents: Generated using E.U. Copernicus Marine Service Information (doi:10.48670/moi-00016); U.S. IOOS HF Radar Network (HFRNet).
  • Wind and waves: Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0); models © NOAA NCEP, ECMWF, Météo-France, DWD and others.
  • Depth: NOAA NCEI ETOPO 2022 (doi:10.25921/fd45-gt74).
  • Buoys: NOAA National Data Buoy Center. Tides: NOAA CO-OPS; Secretaría de Marina (SEMAR), México.
  • Charted features: NOAA Office of Coast Survey ENC Direct (not for navigation). Undersea feature names: IHO-IOC GEBCO Gazetteer (IHO DCDB / NOAA NCEI). Artificial reefs: Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission.
  • Base map: © OpenMapTiles © OpenStreetMap contributors, served by OpenFreeMap.

MAREA is independent and is not endorsed by NOAA, NASA, the European Union, Copernicus, Open-Meteo, SEMAR, FWC or GEBCO.

Each provider's licence and terms apply to its data. MAREA shows the source and time of every value in the app.
