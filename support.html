Troubleshooting

ProblemTry this
"No SST provider available"Check the internet connection, then Settings › Refresh now. Status details are under Conditions › Sea temperature.
Map stays blankUpdate "Android System WebView" and Chrome from the Play Store, then restart MAREA.
GPS dot missingAllow location: Android Settings › Apps › MAREA › Permissions › Location › "Allow only while using the app". Turn on device location, and go outside for a fix.
Find Fish says "bathymetry pending"Depth data for this area is still downloading. Wait, and it retries automatically.
Find Fish finds nothingZoom in further, pick another mode or species, or the area may not match the species' preferences today. That is a valid result.
App slow or closes on an older phoneClose other apps. Use Wi-Fi. Report your phone model to marea.beyondthesurface@gmail.com.
Using too much dataUse Wi-Fi; avoid leaving MAREA open on mobile data for long sessions.
Want to delete everythingAndroid Settings › Apps › MAREA › Storage › Clear storage (or uninstall).
