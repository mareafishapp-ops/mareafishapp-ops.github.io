Privacy Policy — MAREA — Beyond the Surface

Effective date: 2026-10-27 App: MAREA — Beyond the Surface (Android package com.marea.beyondthesurface) Developer: Fenris Diagnostics LLC, Wyoming, United States (shown on Google Play as Ocean Science Dev) Privacy contact: marea.beyondthesurface@gmail.com

1. Summary

  • MAREA has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The developer runs no server and receives no data from the app.
  • To show ocean data, MAREA asks public scientific and mapping services for data about the area you are looking at. Sometimes this includes your location, for example when you tap "SST near me" (section 3).
  • Your settings, saved spots and downloaded data stay on your phone.

2. Information stored only on your device

MAREA keeps the following in its private app storage on your phone. The developer cannot access it, and it is not copied to cloud backup or to a new phone:

  • Settings: units, layers, selected species and fishing mode, the last map position and zoom, and an optional manual location you set yourself.
  • Saved spots and waypoints you create.
  • Downloaded data cache: satellite grids, depth data, structures, tide tables.
  • Diagnostic logs: a crash log and a timing log. They are not sent anywhere. The timing log holds timings only, no coordinates.
  • Optional server address and key, only if you enter one in Settings.

How to delete it: Android Settings › Apps › MAREA › Storage › Clear storage, or uninstall MAREA. Either removes everything listed above.

3. Information sent to third-party data providers

MAREA has to request data from the providers below to work. Each request reaches the provider directly from your phone and includes:

  • The map area or point the data is for. This can be the area you are viewing, a point you tapped, or your GPS position when you use "SST near me" or follow your position on the map. Forecast requests send coordinates to about 11 m precision.
  • Standard internet information sent with any web request: your IP address (which can show your approximate location), the time, and basic app or browser information.

MAREA does not send your name, contacts, account details, advertising ID or device identifiers to these providers. MAREA does not let them use your data for advertising. Each provider's handling of request information is set by its own privacy policy.

4. Location permission

MAREA asks for location permission only while the app is in use. It uses your location to show your position on the map, follow you, measure distances to structures and fishing spots, and show sea conditions near you. MAREA does not use location in the background and does not request background-location permission. You can choose approximate or precise location, or deny it. MAREA still works without it: you can browse the map or set a manual location. Android's location services (on many phones, Google Play services) determine the position under your device settings.

5. What MAREA does not do

MAREA does not sell data, show ads, use advertising IDs, build profiles, use analytics or crash-reporting services, or require an account. It does not collect contacts, photos, files, messages or health data.

6. Children

MAREA is a general-audience fishing tool. It is not directed to children under 13 and knowingly collects no personal information from children.

7. Security

All connections to data providers use encrypted HTTPS. Local data stays in Android's private app storage and is excluded from cloud backup. No method is perfectly secure.

8. Retention

The developer holds no data from you, so it retains none. Data on your device stays until you delete it (section 2). Downloaded caches are replaced as newer data arrives. Third-party providers may keep request logs under their own policies.

9. Your choices and rights

You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in Android settings, and you can delete all local data as described in section 2. The developer holds no personal data about you, so there is nothing for it to give you, correct or delete. Questions or requests: marea.beyondthesurface@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA, Canada PIPEDA, Australia Privacy Act), you may have further rights. Contact us and we will respond within the time those laws require.

10. Safety notice

MAREA shows scientific estimates and observations that can be old, incomplete or wrong. It is not a navigation chart or a safety tool and does not guarantee fish or catches. Always check official charts, weather warnings and local fishing regulations.

11. Changes

Fenris Diagnostics LLC will post any change to this policy at https://mareafishapp-ops.github.io/privacy.html with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
